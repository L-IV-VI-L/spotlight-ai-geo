How to read the audit.

This example simulates the same question three times on each of three AI engines. Switch views to inspect the answer, citations, and mentions.

AI answer

Start with the venues the answer recommends and why. This response names two restaurants and explains private-room capacity and how to book.

Citations

Check which pages the answer cites. Here they include a restaurant’s booking page and a third-party dining guide. Track brand mentions and page citations separately.

Brand mentions

Read counts by engine. In this example, 1 / 3 means one of three responses from that engine names the target brand.

From finding to fix.

The question depends on two details: room for eight and Friday booking. Start by checking whether both are easy to find on the restaurant’s site.

Update the group dining page

List capacity by room, group booking times, and a reservation link. Check that the restaurant site, booking platform, and dining guides agree.

Retest the same question

After the update, repeat the question in the same market, language, and engines. Compare which responses name the restaurant and whether they cite its booking page.
