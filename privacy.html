Privacy & contact

Your enquiry, under your control.

How this website handles the information you enter.

Enquiry information

The enquiry form prepares your brand, website, market, email and goals in your browser. It does not submit that information to a server. Selecting the email action opens your email app with a draft addressed to hello@hashmeta.ai; the information is sent when you send that email.

Local preferences

The website remembers your language choice locally. Case-search terms appear in the URL; this browser tab temporarily remembers your case-list return position. Enquiry content is not saved to browser storage.

External links & fonts

Evidence links open third-party or company source pages. Web fonts are requested from Google Fonts. Those services receive standard connection information under their own policies.

Contact

For questions about an enquiry you have emailed, contact hello@hashmeta.ai.
