GEO basics

What is generative engine optimization?

GEO starts with the questions users ask AI. See which brands the answers mention and which pages they cite, then decide what information to make easier to find and verify.

View sample audit→ Common questions

How to read AI visibility

Track mentions, citations, and competitors separately to see where an answer exposes a gap.

Brand mention rate
The share of sampled answers that name the target brand. If two of five answers mention it, the mention rate is 40%.
Cited sources
Record the pages the AI lists as sources. An answer may name the brand and cite a third-party review or industry guide.
Competitor coverage
Check which competitors recur on the same questions and which facts the answers use to explain those recommendations.

How does GEO relate to SEO?

SEO helps people find pages in search results. GEO examines how AI answers describe a brand and which sources support those answers. Clear, accessible pages support both.

Which questions should I start with?

Start with questions your users ask repeatedly. Include conditions that affect their choice, such as price, service area, or compatibility, in the language they use in that market.

What information should I improve first?

Start with facts the answers omit or get wrong. A restaurant can publish room capacity and booking rules. A software provider can clarify supported integrations and the plans that include them. Put those facts on the pages users look for.

Why repeat the same question?

Answers and citations can vary across runs. Keep the question, language, market, and engine fixed, then compare mentions and citations across multiple samples.
